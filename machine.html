Three things it does differently

Expectancy first

Results rank by average profit per trade, not win rate. A 92% win rate that loses money sorts to the bottom, where it belongs.

Conditions, not signals

You build a setup out of named market conditions and test whether that combination has ever paid. It doesn't tell you what to buy this morning.

It will tell you no

Most setups don't work. The tool is built to show you that quickly, which is the entire value. Software that always finds an edge isn't finding one.
